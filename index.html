Hold My Beer

Grab beers from the fridge and race to the living room couch without spilling them.

You always run forward. The right stick steers left and right; push it up to go faster and down to slow down.

Keep your tray level. Tilt your phone to keep the bubble in the green. In the red, beer spills. Hit the edge and you lose everything.

No tilt sensor? A balance stick appears on the left instead.

Dodge the clutter. Crashing bounces you back and knocks a beer loose. It slides away and can be picked up again after 3 seconds.

Drink a beer for a speed boost and a few seconds of smashing straight through things. You'll get drunker, and drunk trays wobble a lot more.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, A and D balance, space drinks.
